The Problem

⚠️

Quality Risk

Undetected wood defects lead to structural failures, wasted material, and costly customer returns. Manual inspection is slow and subjective.

🕒

Slow Inspection

Human inspectors can only examine a limited volume per shift, creating bottlenecks in production and leaving defects undetected.

💵

High Cost

Missed defects result in expensive rework, rejected shipments, and damage to brand reputation in the wood products industry.

The Solution

🎥 Image-Based Detection

Upload a wood surface image. The system processes it with a YOLOv8 model and draws bounding boxes on each defect in real time.

Launch →

📊 Defect Analytics

Per-image defect count, type distribution, and confidence scores — all presented in an intuitive dashboard for quality control decisions.

Dataset

Large Scale Wood Surface Defects Dataset

Kaggle — nomihsa965

  • 4,000 annotated images (3,200 train / 400 val / 400 test)
  • 8 defect classes: Quartzity, Live_Knot, Marrow, Resin, Dead_Knot, Knot_with_crack, Knot_missing, Crack
  • YOLOv8 bounding box format
  • 2800x1024 resolution
  • CC BY 4.0

Training

Trained with YOLOv8n (nano) on Google Colab T4 GPU for 20 epochs with class-aware augmentation on minority classes (Quartzity, Marrow, Knot_missing). See notebooks/wood_defect_yolov8_training.ipynb for details.

Citation
